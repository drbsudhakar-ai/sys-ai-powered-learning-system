SYS BRAND ASSET FAMILY
========================

Official brand:
SYS - Strengthen Your Skills
Tagline: Shape Your Successful Future.

Frozen visual direction:
Blue + Royal Purple

Recommended digital palette:
- Deep Academic Navy: #102A72
- Primary Blue: #1769E0
- Royal Purple: #6D28D9
- Supporting Bright Purple: #8B5CF6
- White: #FFFFFF

Branding rule:
Use Blue + Royal Purple consistently across the SYS website, dashboards,
AI Lecturer interfaces, generated educational documents, reports,
certificates, and future mobile applications.

Do not use Orange as a primary SYS brand accent. Functional UI colors such
as green/success, red/error, and amber/warning may still be used where
their semantic meaning is required.
