# SYS Brand Asset Family

Official frozen identity for **SYS — Strengthen Your Skills**.

Supporting line: **Strengthen Your Skills**

Tagline: **Shape Your Successful Future.**

## Brand meaning

- Navy `S`: books, knowledge and structured learning.
- Gold `Y`: a joyful graduate whose raised arms embrace learning and AI.
- Purple `S`: AI-powered guidance and future skills.

## Mandatory application coverage

Branding must be present in:

- every public page, authenticated screen and role dashboard;
- admin, faculty and student navigation shells;
- login, access-denied, loading and error states;
- question papers, assessment reports and answer keys;
- lecture notes, handouts, shortcut summaries and generated materials;
- performance, remedial, mastery and learning-journey reports;
- certificates, report cards and downloadable exports;
- notification templates and institutional email layouts.

## Screen rules

1. Use one shared `SYSHeader`, one shared `SYSFooter` and centralized brand tokens.
2. Desktop headers use a horizontal lockup; narrow mobile headers use the compact symbol.
3. Do not distort, recolor, rotate, outline or add effects to the logo.
4. Preserve clear space equal to at least 25% of the graduate head diameter.
5. Never use the master lockup below 220 px wide; switch to the compact mark.
6. Keep page-specific titles outside the logo artwork.
7. Maintain WCAG-readable contrast for all surrounding text and controls.

## Generated-document rules

1. The first page carries the master or header lockup in the header area.
2. Every subsequent page carries a compact header mark or footer identity.
3. The light watermark is centered behind content at approximately 6–10% visual opacity.
4. Watermarks must never reduce question, formula, table, diagram or answer readability.
5. Footers should include `SYS — Strengthen Your Skills`, the artifact title/type, page number and generated/version date when appropriate.
6. Question papers and answer keys must include assessment/version identifiers.
7. Reports and generated materials must retain the same brand treatment in PDF and print output.

## Watermark placement

- Default: centered on the page, behind content, no rotation.
- Dense question papers: use a smaller centered mark or footer watermark.
- Certificates: use the larger centered watermark at low opacity.
- Do not tile watermarks or place them over answer fields, diagrams or signatures.

## Official colours

- Midnight Navy: `#082A66`
- Achievement Gold: `#F6A800`
- Royal Purple: `#6E2CBF`
- Surface White: `#FFFFFF`
- Primary Ink: `#111827`
- Secondary Ink: `#4B5563`

Use gradients only for digital promotional artwork. Product UI, reports and print assets should use flat colours by default.

## Governance

The concept, symbol relationships and wording are frozen. New variants may change only size, layout, background treatment or monochrome rendering. Any change to letter meaning, colours, wording, graduate pose, book placement or AI placement requires an explicit brand revision decision.
